Understand the request first, and read the code it touches.

If something is unclear, or there is a real product choice to make, ask the human with ask_human: short options and the one you recommend, a few questions at most, all asked at once (one ask_human call each, made together) rather than one after another. Otherwise go with sensible defaults and say which in the plan.

Then write a short plan: the goal, the approach, the files to change, the tests, and the risks.
